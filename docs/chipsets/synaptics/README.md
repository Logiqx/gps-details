## Synaptics

### Overview

Since mid-2020, Synaptics have been producing chipsets such as the SYN4778, after [acquiring certain assets](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2020/07/07/2058930/0/en/Synaptics-to-Acquire-Rights-to-Broadcom-s-Wireless-IoT-Connectivity-Business.html) from Broadcom.

The SYN4778 (equivalent of the BCM4778) has since been identified in Garmin watches, starting with the fēnix 8 in Aug 2024.

The latest Synaptics GNSS chipsets can be found on their [website](https://www.synaptics.com/products/wireless), and also on the [EDOM Technology](https://www.edomtech.com/en/product-detail/synaptics-gps-gnss-solutions/) website.

#### SYN4778

The official Synaptics [announcement](https://www.synaptics.com/company/news/synaptics-launches-syn4778-lowest-power-smallest-most-accurate-gnss-ic-for-iot-devices) for the SYN4778 was in Jan 2023, and appears to be a re-badged [Broadcom](../broadcom/README.md) BCM4778 from Sep 2021.

The SYN4778 was first used in the Garmin [fēnix 8](../../devices/garmin/watches/fenix.md) in Aug 2024, and  [COROS](../../devices/coros/chipsets/README.md) Pace Pro in Nov 2024.

It is worth noting that some Garmin watches contain the SYN47782CX. The "2CX" may denote specific packaging, temperature grade, or tape-and-reel configuration. This is likely detailed in the Synaptics ordering guide, but requires accessing their NDA datasheet.
