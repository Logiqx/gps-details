## Amazfit GTR 4

### Overview

The Amazfit GTR 4 was released in Sep 2022 uses an Airoha GNSS chipset, according to the deep dive teardown by [TechInsights](https://electronics360.globalspec.com/article/19592/techinsights-teardown-amazfit-gts-4) in May 2023.

This will almost certainly by the [Airoha AG3335M](https://www.airoha.com/products/p/A0Dmm0pijWW3MScb).
